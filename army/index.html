THE PROBLEM

Why We Need Clear Rules

Most holders won't swap

BONE, LEASH, and TREAT holders are largely inactive. Many will simply choose not to participate, meaning very few people will actually exchange.

No ratio satisfies everyone

Without complete rules, a new token sitting alongside old ones only splits the Shib community further. We need a clear, fair, executable framework.

Core principle: Use market mechanisms instead of top-down decisions. No forced swaps, no token burns — let the new token naturally replace old ones through price action and arbitrage.

CORE RULES

How It Works

01

Zero Burn

No tokens are burned during the exchange. Old tokens remain in the system as the basis for reversible conversion. Nothing is destroyed — everything is transformable.

02

Reversible Exchange (Original Swappers Only)

Anyone who completes an exchange can convert ARMY back to the original token — as long as they still hold the ARMY. This right is non-transferable: if the original swapper sells their ARMY, the new buyer cannot swap back.

STEP 1
Swap old → ARMY
STEP 2
Hold or sell ARMY
STEP 3
If still holding: swap back
03

24-Hour Cooldown (Both Directions)

Every exchange operation — whether swapping old tokens to ARMY or converting ARMY back — requires a 24-hour cooldown. This prevents rapid-fire manipulation and ensures deliberate decision-making. No new exchange can be initiated during the cooldown period.

04

Price Protection via Arbitrage

This mechanism ensures ARMY's price never falls below the old tokens. If ARMY drops below parity, original swappers can buy ARMY cheaply, swap back to old tokens, and sell at a profit — market forces automatically close the gap.

Arbitrage Flow:
Buy ARMY (cheap) → Swap back to old token → Sell old token (profit)
⚠ Original swappers bear price volatility risk during the 24h cooldown. The contract does not guarantee market price at conversion time.

MARKET DYNAMICS

Natural Replacement Logic

No forced migration. No burns. The new token replaces old ones through pure market forces.

1

Price rises → Old token holders are incentivized to exchange for ARMY

2

Arbitrage safety net → If old tokens outperform, original swappers can reverse and profit

3

Gradual shift → More holders exchange and choose to keep ARMY, old tokens exit circulation

4

Natural unification → ARMY becomes the de facto token through market adoption
